Free, for good.

Wemmick costs nothing to download or to use, and it stays that way.

No price

No trial, no subscription, no limit on meetings or minutes, and no account to sign up for.

Open source

The code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it, change it, or build it yourself.

Nothing to sell

No ads and no analytics. Your meetings aren’t the product, since they never leave your Mac.

One optional cost

If you choose Claude to write notes, you use your own Anthropic API key, usually a cent or two a meeting. The model on your Mac is free.

Up and running in minutes.

Wemmick needs a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later, and about 16 GB of memory.

1. Download

Open the disk image and drag Wemmick to Applications. It’s signed and notarized by Apple.

2. First launch

Wemmick downloads the Python packages it transcribes with, about 1.5 GB, once.

3. Setup

Tell it your name, add a free Hugging Face token for the speaker model, and choose where notes and to-dos go.

4. Record

Drop in a recording. Name the speakers once, and Wemmick knows their voices from then on.

Named for a clerk who kept work at work.

In Great Expectations, John Wemmick writes everything down at the office, then goes home to his little castle in Walworth, pulls up the drawbridge, and none of it follows him. Your meetings stay on your Mac the same way.
